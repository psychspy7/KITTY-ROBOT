# Review R2.1 with the detachable tail

Download `index.html` and open it in Chrome or Edge. It shows six actual saved CAD views immediately: front corner, front, rear corner, side, interior and exploded tail joint. Choose **Side** for the tail silhouette or **Tail joint** for its keyed connection. **Enable rotatable 3D** loads the matching embedded draft assembly mesh.

`saved-views.html` is the smaller image-only version. All six native view buttons work without JavaScript or WebGL. `overview.png`, `side.png` and `tail-joint.png` are direct CAD images for quick review.

The current body is retained. The revision adds a curved detachable tail, an integral rear socket with a proposed M3 cross screw, bevelled/recessed passive ear details and two shallow lower-bezel grooves. The exploded joint is displaced 35 mm rearward; the screw is omitted. Remove the cross screw before pulling the real tail rearward.

These files are generated by `../make_review.py` from the checked draft mesh and matching CAD PNGs. Final STL release follows the user's shape decision and the unresolved component/printer fit checks in the parent README. This review does not start a print.
