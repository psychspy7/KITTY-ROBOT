# Sentinel R2 — appearance proposal, fit pending

User requested a more distinctive, polished robot and a single final STL set after design lock. This image proposes a warm-white faceted serviceable body, charcoal chassis, landscape phone face, restrained cat-like upper bezel corners and amber eye graphics. A short broad neck and low battery compartment favor stability. Two drive wheels plus caster; no arms or walking mechanism. Ears are passive frame details, not actuators.

Approximate visual envelope is a20cm-wide base and28–32cm overall height, using65mm wheels only as an illustration assumption. **Do not manufacture from these values.** Actual phone, wheels, motors and printer bed may change them. The front sensor/camera-shaped inset is an appearance placeholder; it does not establish a purchased camera or sensor. The actual phone camera must have an unobstructed forward view. Standard sonar and ToF need different openings/mounts.

Proposed printable modules: base, fitted motor mounts, low battery tray/strap points, removable split body cover, short neck, padded phone cradle with retention and camera/USB access, separate sensor bracket. A complete draft implementation now exists in [complete-draft/README.md](complete-draft/README.md), with 13 separate proposed printed pieces in the R2.1 tail revision and whole-robot assembly exports. This is not a physically fitted release. Print only necessary, fit-checked pieces. Keep battery, switch/cutoff, USB and driver service access reachable.

Required before final CAD: actual motor type/dimensions/shaft location, wheel diameter/width/hub, caster installed height, phone W/H/T/camera/port positions, controller/driver/sensor footprints, battery dimensions, servo case/horn and usable printer bed. Phone load on a servo requires a torque/load review; do not use a small unknown servo as a whole-phone neck by assumption.

Latest user input selects BO motors, HC-SR04, TB6612 and OPPO A57. During complete drafting, the user reported 62 mm wheel diameter, motor length/width 61 × 20 mm and phone 164 × 76 × 8 mm. ESP32 is primary controller; Uno is spare unless a concrete need emerges. Use these reported dimensions while keeping motor height/shaft, wheel hub, case/camera/ports, caster, AA holder and printer constraints unresolved. OPPO A57 has multiple versions; verify the actual arrangement before fit approval.

Existing compact N20 and companion BO designs are preserved in the separate consolidated handoff. They are not interchangeable or the R2 design. A new fitted revision should use the selected real drivetrain, include STL+STEP+source, assembly view, quantities, mesh checks and fit checks. No new R2 STL is being called final while these inputs are missing.

R2.1 design review: the user selected keeping the current body with a detachable tail and small refinements. The draft adds a curved rear tail with a keyed socket and proposed M3 cross screw, plus bevelled/recessed passive ear details and lower phone-bezel grooves. Appearance remains awaiting the user’s review; final print files follow that decision and the outstanding fit checks.

V3 is now a separate refinement in [../v3/README.md](../v3/README.md), retaining this body family and reported hardware sizes while shortening the neck, reducing the tail projection and refining sensor, vent, bezel and chest details. R2.1 remains available for comparison. Final appearance and physical fit remain pending.
