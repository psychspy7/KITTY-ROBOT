# V3 Signature design intent

Build a recognizable KITTY silhouette around the purchased-parts report: landscape phone face, passive cat ears, compact fixed neck, warm light faceted body, dark chassis, two wheels and an upward curled detachable tail. The aim is a coherent character that can be explained and serviced during a demonstration.

The R2.1 body footprint is retained. V3 lowers the proposed phone position, reduces modeled plastic volume, rounds the sensor/service details and brings the tail closer. It adds no printed modules, arms, walking mechanism or servo phone load. The final Signature pass turns the small chest mark into a broad folded collar with a bevelled rim. A faceted cuff repeats that gesture on the fixed neck, while two rear tie eyes provide proposed cable-tidying points. A small arrow below the tail cross-screw entry communicates rearward removal after the screw is removed. All four details belong to existing pieces; the total remains 13 printed parts. The neck attachment holes, main cable route and tail key/bore are retained.

The phone, wheel diameter and motor length/width are user-reported. Other interfaces and print constraints remain assumptions. Colours are a proposed finish; the aqua eyes are screen placeholders. Geometric volume savings are not mass/strength/stability or print-time results.

Demo value depends on showing real sensing, controlled motion and reliable stopping. The CAD preview and face graphics are design evidence; they do not establish implemented autonomous hardware. This revision is ready for an appearance decision, followed by actual fit and print preparation.

The final pass follows the ranked repository-grounded review in `../../docs/ideation/2026-10-23-kitty-v3-final-detail-ideation.md`. Optional fit docks and new structural bridges were deferred. The selected details add 0.80% solid volume to the previous V3 and retain a 4.23% reduction against R2.1; neither figure is a measured print or performance result.
